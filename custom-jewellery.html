How It Works

Two Ways to Begin Your Story

Whether you have an heirloom sketch or a Pinterest photo, we make the bespoke journey effortless.

1. Send Us a Photo or Sketch

Found a design you love online, in an editorial magazine, or inherited a vintage piece you want recreated? Send the picture via WhatsApp or form. We evaluate metal weight, stones, and provide exact estimate within 2 hours.

☎ Send Photo via WhatsApp

2. Describe Your Concept & Stones

Not sure about technical details? Tell us the occasion (bridal, anniversary, daily luxury), preferred metal finish (Rhodium silver, Rose gold, Yellow gold), and stone preference (Emerald, Tourmaline, Diamond). We create 3D renders together.
