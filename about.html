About HSB jewells

For the Diva. For the Feminine. For Her.

HSB jewells — Her Story Book — exists for one reason: to make jewellery feel personal again.

Our Philosophy

Not Just Jewellery. The Weight of Your Presence.

We believe jewellery should say something — about who you are, where you come from, and who you're becoming. That's why every HSB piece blends old craftsmanship — kundan, meenakari, temple work — with a new, modern styling sense. It's about the diva in you, the femininity you carry, and the feelings only a piece truly yours can hold.

Where We Are

Rooted in Two Cities, Reaching All of India

HSB jewells is present in Mumbai and Gujarat, with pieces crafted and delivered across India. Wherever you are, your story travels with you.

  • Ahmedabad, Gujarat
  • Mumbai, Maharashtra
  • Shipping across India
